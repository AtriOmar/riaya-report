\chapter*{General Introduction}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{General Introduction}

\paragraph{General Context.}
The digital transformation of the healthcare sector has become a major strategic issue worldwide, and particularly in Tunisia. The growing demand for faster, more accessible, and more efficient medical services has encouraged the emergence of digital platforms that aim to simplify the relationship between patients and healthcare professionals. In this context, information and communication technologies, especially artificial intelligence and real-time communication tools, offer new opportunities to modernize medical appointment management and improve access to care.

\paragraph{Problem Statement.}
Despite the increasing use of the Internet and mobile applications, many patients in Tunisia still face significant difficulties when trying to book a medical appointment. Traditional booking methods often require phone calls during working hours, physical presence, or the use of web platforms that are not always adapted to all users. In addition, language barriers, lack of digital literacy, and the urgent nature of certain medical situations can make the process slow and stressful. Existing solutions generally require patients to create an account or use a web portal, which excludes a part of the population and does not fully address the need for immediate, simple, and multilingual assistance.

\paragraph{Proposed Solution.}
To address these challenges, the company Pengguin, led by John Jang, has developed \textbf{Riaya}, an AI-powered doctor appointment platform based in Tunisia. Riaya is designed around an ``AI-first'' and ``voice-first'' approach: patients do not need a web portal or a mobile application. Instead, they interact with an intelligent voice assistant through a simple phone call and receive follow-up messages via WhatsApp. The AI assistant can communicate in English, French, and Tunisian Arabic, helping patients describe their needs, choose a speciality, provide their location, and book an appointment with the most suitable doctor. Doctors, on the other hand, benefit from a complete web dashboard to manage their practice, appointments, patients, invoices, medical documents, and WhatsApp communication. Administrators can verify doctors, monitor live AI calls, and supervise the platform.

\paragraph{Objectives of the Report.}
The main objective of this report is to present the Riaya project from both a functional and a technical perspective. More specifically, this report aims to:
\begin{itemize}
    \item present the context of the project and the needs of the different actors;
    \item describe the functional and non-functional requirements of the platform;
    \item analyze existing solutions and position Riaya in relation to them;
    \item explain the project management methodology, particularly Kanban, and the modeling diagrams used;
    \item detail the technical architecture, the technology stack, the implementation choices, and the deployment strategy;
    \item provide relevant screenshots and descriptions of the main features of the platform.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Structure of the Report.}
This report is organized into three main chapters. The first chapter introduces the host company Pengguin, presents the Riaya product, identifies the functional and non-functional requirements, compares Riaya with existing solutions, and provides a global BPMN model of the project. The second chapter focuses on the project management methodology, especially Kanban, including its definition, advantages, disadvantages, and a comparative table with other methodologies. It also lists and presents the main modeling diagrams that can be used for the project. The third chapter is dedicated to the technical architecture and implementation of Riaya, covering the technology stack, detailed descriptions of screenshots, security aspects, and the deployment pipeline. Finally, a general conclusion summarizes the work, discusses the challenges encountered, and presents future perspectives.
